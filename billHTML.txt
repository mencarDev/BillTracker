 GENERAL BILL by <span>Stark, Berfield; (CO-INTRODUCERS) Cobb, Eskamani, Gerwig, Plasencia</span>        </p>         <p class="more">            Local Housing Assistance Plans; Authorizes county or eligible municipality to expend certain funds on lot rental assistance for mobile home owners for specified time period; requires counties &amp; eligible municipalities to develop certain strategies in their local housing assistance plans; provides that lot rental assistance is approved home ownership activity for certain purposes; authorizes certain funds to be provided to mobile home owners for rehabilitation &amp; emergency repairs; removes prohibition that only certain percentage of funds may be used for manufactured housing.        </p>        <div id="snapshot" class="grid-100 top">
